Building the sdist/wheel with current toolchains (setuptools>=66 via the pyproject build backend) rejects the metadata; older setuptools produce mangled Requires-Dist entries for pyyaml and jinja2.

`setup.py:12-16` hands `f.readlines()` straight to install_requires while `requirements.txt:1-2` carries pip-format inline comments ("pyyaml  # … MIT", "jinja2 # … 3 Clause BSD"). Verified 2026-10-23: `packaging.Requirement` raises InvalidRequirement on both lines.

**Fix:** strip comments and whitespace when loading `requirements.txt`, or move to comment-free PEP 508 lines.

Impact: touches package metadata generation; prod today inline-comment lines from requirements.txt reach `install_requires` and modern setuptools rejects the metadata, expected: clean PEP 508 requirements. Files: `setup.py`, `requirements.txt`.
