A plain GET of the kernel, initrd, or image-download endpoints can permanently strip a jobserver from an image: on any non-2xx fetch status the view deletes the association and saves the image row (`systems/views_noauth.py:95-100`, `systems/views_noauth.py:223-228`, at `6afb414`). If a jobserver is momentarily busy or restarting, an unauthenticated read whose identity is a spoofable header permanently deletes the relation.

**Fix:** never persist association changes from unauthenticated read paths; treat jobserver health as a separate authenticated maintenance task with retry thresholds, and mark servers unhealthy in cache instead of deleting relations.

Impact: touches the unauthenticated kernel/initrd/image-download endpoints; prod today one failed fetch permanently removes the jobserver from the image, expected: reads never persist association changes. Fix in flight: #343. Files: `systems/views_noauth.py`.
