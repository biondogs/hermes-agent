No-argument `connect` reads only two keys (`apikey`, `mprovURL` under `global`) and silently ignores everything else: a config with a typo like `apiKeys:` or `mprovurl:` connects with an empty auth header, and the only symptom is 401s on every request (`src/mash/app.py:180-188`, at `36b6a5e`). There is no validation step at load time, no warning for unknown keys, and no schema documented (see #33 for the crash side of the same gap).

**Fix:** Validate the loaded config against the known key set at load_config time, emit a warning naming unrecognized keys, and reject connect with a clear error when the needed keys are missing.
