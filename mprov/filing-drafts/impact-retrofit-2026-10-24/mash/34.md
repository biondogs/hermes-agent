`shlex.split` has no maxsplit parameter — its second positional parameter is `comments`. Passing 2 enables comment handling, so any `#` in the remainder starts a comment and the rest of the line vanishes: a `foreach` body whose item contains `#` is silently truncated (`src/mash/app.py:362-365`, at `36b6a5e`).

**Fix:** Call `shlex.split` with only the string (comments stay disabled), or split the line with a partition-based parse that takes the first token, the keyword, and the remainder verbatim.

Impact: touches `foreach` line parsing; prod today everything after a `#` in the item list vanishes, expected: hash characters stay inside tokens. Files: `src/mash/app.py`.

Severity: S3 — hash-bearing values (passwords, URLs with fragments) disappear mid-command with no warning.
