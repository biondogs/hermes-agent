Fixes #34

`shlex.split(arg, 2)` passes `2` as shlex's `comments` parameter — shlex has no `maxsplit`. With comments enabled, everything after a `#` was discarded, so `foreach n in c1 c2 #see-infra` silently looped over a truncated list.

## Change
- Use `split(None, 2)`: the intended three-part split, with `#` preserved inside tokens — the remainder is split on plain spaces downstream anyway. Adds `tests/test_foreach.py`.

## Impact

Touches `foreach` line parsing. Before: a `#` in the item list silently truncates it; after: hash characters stay inside tokens. Files: `src/mash/app.py`, `tests/test_foreach.py`.
