Fixes #24

`bmc power <action> <node>` failed when the mPCC URL was typed in its natural form — `connect http://127.0.0.1:8080 apikey KEY`, no trailing slash — every power command dying with `Failed to parse: '127.0.0.1:8080power' is not a valid host or port`. `src/mash/plugins/bmc.py` concatenates `power/{action}/...` straight onto `mprovURL`, so only URLs ending in `/` happened to join.

## The fix
Normalize `mprovURL` once in `do_connect` (`rstrip("/") + "/"`) before the connection attempt — the single point every connect form (apikey, user/password, config) passes through. Slash-terminated URLs behave exactly as before.

## Impact

Touches the connect-time URL. Before: without a trailing slash every `bmc power` command failed on a malformed host join; after: the URL is normalized once, slash-terminated forms behave exactly as before. Files: `src/mash/app.py`, `tests/test_bmc.py`, `tests/test_connect.py`.

## Testing
