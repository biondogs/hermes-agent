Fixes #172

With `imageList` configured, image status updates claimed every pending job in the queue as running, not just the configured image's job. `image_update.handle_jobs()` built the filter by stringifying a JSON object (`{"imageId":"..."}`) and `update_job_status()` appended it raw after `jobs/?`; the mPCC cannot parse that key, so the reply contained every pending job and the claim loop patched them all.

## Change
- Query this job server's pending image-update jobs the way `image_delete` already does (`jobserver`/`module`/`status=1`), and add an optional `params_filter` to `update_job_status` that narrows the claim client-side on parsed job params. Existing callers are unaffected.

## Impact
Touches the image-update job claim path. Before: with `imageList` set, status updates claimed every pending job in the queue. After: only this job server's matching image job is claimed. Files: `src/mprov_jobserver/app.py`, `src/mprov_jobserver/plugins/image_update.py`, `tests/conftest.py`, `tests/test_app.py`, `tests/test_image_update.py`.

## Testing
`test_configured_image_list_only_claims_its_own_jobs` binds the real `update_job_status` and asserts the claim patch set: on base the whole pending queue (`jobs/9/`, `jobs/10/`, `jobs/11/`); on the fix only `jobs/9/`. Plus a unit test for `params_filter`. Suite failure set identical to base.
