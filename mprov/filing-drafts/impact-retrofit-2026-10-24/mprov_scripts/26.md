Importing a spreadsheet-edited (CRLF-terminated) CSV silently writes hostnames, MACs and IPs with stray carriage returns into the mPCC — verified by execution (ip=[172.20.1.10\r]) — and any embedded space splits one CSV row into two garbage records. The import reports success; provisioning those nodes later fails in confusing ways. (Same script as the silent-wrong-IPs finding in biondogs/mprov_scripts#13 — distinct defect.)

`tools/import_systems_csv.sh:68-73`: `for entry in \`cat $filename\`` word expansion over the file plus `IFS=',' read -ra DATA`, no field normalization.

**Fix:** read records line-by-line, strip CR and surrounding whitespace from every field, and reject rows whose field count is wrong.
